# Build-a-Multicloud-Operations-Lab-with-NCP-MCI-7.5-Practice-Questions
The Nutanix Certified Professional - Multicloud Infrastructure 7.5 exam covers administrative decisions across clusters, workloads, recovery, and daily operations. Use CertQueen [NCP-MCI-7.5 exam questions](https://www.certqueen.com/NCP-MCI-7.5.html) alongside ECA training to identify which decisions you can explain and which tasks need another lab session.

One workload can connect deployment, migration, recovery, and monitoring in a practical study project.

## Exam details for your operations lab

- **Length and duration** - 75 single-response and multiple-response questions, 120 minutes
- **Passing standard** - 3000 on a scale from 1000 to 6000
- **Language and delivery** - English or Japanese, remotely proctored or at selected test centers
- **Fee** - USD 200 per attempt
- **Software scope** - AOS 7.5, AHV 11.0, Prism Central pc.7.5

Passing the 7.5 exam earns the **NCP-MCI 7** certification. The blueprint recommends at least a year of infrastructure experience and comparable Nutanix experience.

## Connect the lab to the blueprint

The four domains cover cluster management, workload management, disaster recovery and protection, and operations and monitoring. Cluster objectives include deployment, Prism Central, networking, storage, and security. Workload objectives cover VMs, containers, storage, conversion, and protection controls.

Recovery objectives address Prism Central backup and restore, PC-based replication and migration requirements, and PE-based protection and failover. Operations objectives include capacity, performance, logs, NCC, and LCM.

## Map the environment before deploying

Draw a small test environment with a cluster, Prism Central, a workload subnet, and a VM. Record the services and configuration details you need before deployment. Use the blueprint's software versions to select the right training material and check feature availability in your lab.

## Follow one VM through its operational lifecycle

Configure the VM, place its disks in a suitable container, and record its network and storage requirements. Design a migration exercise and a recovery test for the same workload. Finish with a short incident report that connects a performance symptom with the observations you collected.

## Review the operational decision

After a practice question, identify the condition that guided your answer. Describe what additional evidence you would collect before making the same decision in an environment you administer. Then change one condition and explain whether your choice changes.

Keep a short log with the relevant objective, the reason you hesitated, and a lab result that would close the gap. Use Nutanix Enterprise Cloud Administration training and the official blueprint to review the task. For any recovery or configuration exercise, define the expected result before you begin and record how you confirmed it.

## Use the project to demonstrate progress

NCP-MCI 7 validates professional skills in administering a Nutanix multicloud environment. The credential is relevant to administrators, engineers, operators, and support personnel who deploy and manage the platform. It remains valid for three years under the reviewed certification guidance.
